# Fixture Collaboration Demo Contract

The Vercel demo is a Current Prototype companion. It gives colleagues and
leads a safe desktop product trial; it is never a paid-release or production
environment.

## Required boundary

- Use only approved local fixtures, verified SwissBlue property/room facts,
  approved English synthetic clients, and clearly fictional transactions.
- Do not configure a live Supabase URL/key, production credential, webhook,
  payment, government, channel, or external connection.
- Do not retain reviewer-entered data after a refresh or reset.
- Label the experience as a fixture-only, non-production collaboration demo.
- Do not present a broken demo as a production fallback or redirect to real
  operational data.
- Do not use the demo to market early-bird subscriptions until the documented
  UI/UX readiness gate has passed.

## Authorisation and release gate

1. The user explicitly approves the Vercel deployment.
2. The fixture build passes and the deployment contains no secret or live
   endpoint configuration.
3. The core journeys pass the documented UI/UX checklist: clear labels,
   professional desktop finish, no journey-blocking layout/readability defect,
   and no dead primary control.
4. A reviewer opens the link in a modern desktop browser and completes the
   published smoke journey at 1440px or larger.
5. The owner records the passing result before sharing the link with a wider
   reviewer group or using it in early-bird marketing.

## Smoke journey

1. Open the demo and recognise the fixture-only boundary and Gulf Hero brand.
2. Enter the seeded preview without providing real credentials.
3. Switch a visible selected-property/role fixture context and confirm no
   unassigned property data is exposed.
4. Open the reservation, rate/availability, housekeeping, cashiering, guest,
   and report workspaces; perform only their clearly local presentation actions.
5. Confirm every tested control completes locally or explains its unavailable
   boundary, and no guest, payment, or external connection is requested.
